You can create a **Lesson Plan** to let your student's keep track of the progress of the course.

Proceed to **Lesson Plan** (Sidebar). 

On this page, there are 2 things you can create:
  1. **Milestones**
  2. **Events**

**Milestones** demarcates and helps you organize your timeline into separate chunks (something like sections).

**Events** are events that you can schedule into your timeline, for example:
  1. Lectures
  2. Tutorials
  3. Recitations
  4. Others

**Notice:** Assessments that are created will also show on the lesson plan automatically! Their positions are determined by their **start dates**.

![7D-1](/img/instructors/7D-1.png)

To create a **Milestone**:
  1. Click **New Milestone**.
  2. Fill in the details.
  3. The **Start at** date is demarcator indicating that the milestone has been reached.
  4. Click **Submit**.

![7D-2](/img/instructors/7D-2.png)

To create a new **Lesson Plan Event**:
  1. Click **New Event**.
  2. Fill in the details.
  3. The **Start at** date determines the event's position on the timeline.
  4. Click **Submit**.

![7D-3](/img/instructors/7D-3.png)
