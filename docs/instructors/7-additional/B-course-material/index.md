title: 7b. Course Materials
---



To manage your course materials, you can organize them into subfolders.
  1. Click on **Materials** on the side bar.
  2. Click on the subfolder icon on the top right.
  3. Fill in the details accordingly. 
  4. Click **Submit**.

![7B-1](/img/instructors/7B-1.png)

Navigate into the specific subfolder to upload file(s).
  1. Click on the **Upload** icon.
  2. Drag and drop relevant file(s) for upload.
  3. Click **Upload**.

![7B-2](/img/instructors/7B-2.png)
