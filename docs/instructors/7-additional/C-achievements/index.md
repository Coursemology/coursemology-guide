You can setup achievements if you have enabled gamification for your course. 
Achievements are badges students can earn if they meet the criteria set.

To create a new **Achievement**:
  1. Click on **Achievements** on the side bar.
  2. Click on the **New Achievement** button on the top right.
  3. Fill in the details accordingly. 
  4. Click **Submit**.

![7C-1](/img/instructors/7C-1.png)

Conditions are prerequisites that the student must possess before being able to attain the achievement.

You can set the condition as follows:
  1. Click on the edit icon.
  2. Click **Add a condition**.

There are 4 types of conditions:
  1. **Achievement** (requires another achievement)
  3. **Assessment** (requires the completion of a certain assessment)
  2. **Level** (requires a certain level)
  4. **Survey** (requires the completion of a certain survey)


![7C-2](/img/instructors/7C-2.gif)

If you have chosen an assessment condition, you will be able to see the new achievement on that assessment page (the achievement will need to be published!).

![7C-3](/img/instructors/7C-3.png)
