title: 4g. Timed Assessment
---

Timed assessment is a useful feature if you want to give students timed practices.
Each student will get an individual timer based on their start time.

To enable timed assessment, go to assessment settings:
1. Check the **Enable Timed Assessment** checkbox.
2. Enter the desired duration.
3. Finally click **Save** to save the settings.

![4G-1](/img/instructors/4G-1.png)

Below is the student view of the timed assessment. 
Student will see his/her individualized timer at the top right hand corner.
![4G-2](/img/instructors/4G-2.png)

When time is up, the submission will be submitted automatically.
